# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee data using **Microsoft Power BI** to understand workforce distribution, employee attrition, performance ratings, job satisfaction, promotion eligibility, and employee tenure.

The dashboard provides interactive insights that help HR teams identify workforce patterns and explore areas that may require attention to improve employee retention.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total employees and employee attrition.
- Understand workforce distribution by gender and department.
- Identify employee performance and job satisfaction levels.
- Analyze employee tenure and job levels.
- Identify employees due for promotion and retrenchment.
- Explore factors associated with employee attrition.
- Provide insights to support HR decision-making.

## 📂 Dataset Information

- **Total Employees:** 1,470
- **Total Columns:** 41
- **Employees Who Left:** 237
- **Current Employees:** 1,233
- **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.1%

The dataset contains employee-related information, including:

- Employee demographics
- Department and job role
- Monthly income
- Job satisfaction
- Performance rating
- Years at company
- Years since last promotion
- Overtime and business travel

## 📈 Key Insights

- The overall employee attrition rate is **16.1%**, with 237 employees leaving the organization.
- Sales Representatives have the highest job-role attrition rate at **39.8%**.
- Employees working overtime have a higher attrition rate (**30.5%**) compared to employees without overtime (**10.4%**).
- Employees who left have an average age of 33.6 years, compared to 37.6 years for employees who stayed.
- Employees who left have an average company tenure of 5.1 years, compared to 7.4 years for employees who stayed.

## 💡 Recommendations

- Strengthen early-tenure employee retention through structured onboarding and regular check-ins.
- Review overtime and workload distribution.
- Focus on job roles with higher attrition rates.
- Improve career development opportunities and internal mobility.
- Monitor employee attrition and engagement indicators regularly.
